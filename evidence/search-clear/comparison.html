What changed and why

The clear button's box was 24 points wide while theme::icon_button allocates its 15-point glyph plus 12 points of padding. egui clamps a centered layout that does not fit to the box's top edge, so the glyph landed 1.5 points below the pill's midline (and equally right of center). The candidate sizes the box to what the button actually allocates. Hit targets are unchanged; only the glyph and the button rect move 1.5 points up-left to true center.
